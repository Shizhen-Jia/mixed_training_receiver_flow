# References and Source Availability

## Supplied research manuscript

Stefan Daniel Tancovici, Shizhen Jia, Marco Mezzavilla, C. Nicolas Barati, and Sundeep Rangan. **Delay–Doppler Synchronization for Distributed Uplink Interference Nulling in LEO Networks.** Supplied manuscript, provided locally as `journal_paper.pdf`.

The supplied PDF does not identify a publication date, venue, DOI, or public manuscript URL. It is not included in this public project repository. The initial plan states its required input/output behavior and mathematics directly, so the grader does not need the PDF to understand the proposed IP.

The principal source locations are Section II-A for the two-branch input model; Section II-B and Figure 1 for acquisition; Equations (6)–(8) for alignment and normalized refinement; Equations (9)–(12) for covariance and noise-normalized cancellation; and Table I / Section IV-A for the simulation settings. Algorithm details not completely specified by the manuscript will be documented as implementation choices, without claiming exact reproduction.
